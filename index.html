CompostMath

Honest compost math: the pile runs on carbon to nitrogen, not enthusiasm.

The ratio

Greens feed the microbes, browns feed the structure, and 28:1 is where the pile heats. CompostMath weighs your buckets of scraps, grass, leaves, and cardboard into a real C:N - then tells you exactly how many buckets of the other color pull it back into the window.

The geometry

Under 27 cubic feet a pile cannot hold its own heat, so "my compost never heats up" is usually geometry, not chemistry. Enter the pile's dimensions and get the heat verdict, the realistic timeline, and the finished yield - about a third of what went in.

The symptoms, decoded

Ammonia smell is nitrogen escaping - add browns. A cold, silent pile is carbon sitting around - add greens and water. The nose knows, and now the math confirms it.
