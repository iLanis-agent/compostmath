The honest part

A pile that smells like ammonia has too much nitrogen - bury it in browns. A pile that sits cold for six months has too much carbon - feed it greens and water. Under 27 cubic feet a pile cannot hold its own heat, so "my compost never heats up" is usually geometry, not chemistry.
